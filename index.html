Fintech Publisher Portfolio · Funded-Account Growth

Which mid-tier publishers can become top-tier — and what to pull to get them there?

Established fintech affiliate programs grow by getting more funded accounts — and better ones — from the publishers they already have. This models a large portfolio, scores every mid-tier publisher on how likely they are to reach top-tier by how closely they match your best publishers' profile and how much headroom they have, and names the levers to pull. Built around funded-account economics: you pay a CPA per funded account, so the game is first-year revenue per account and ROI, not just sign-ups. How the scoring works

Every publisher sits on two axes: funding quality (do their accounts fund, deposit, and retain) and scale (volume). Top-tier publishers are strong on both; the promotion opportunities are the mid-tier publishers strong on one axis with room on the other. Each mid-tier publisher gets a promotion score from how closely they match your top-tier fingerprint — channel, publisher type, audience, geo — and how much headroom they have, with scale and quality weighted evenly, so a big publisher with fixable quality ranks alongside a high-quality one that just needs volume. Because this is a funded-account model, quality drives first-year revenue per account and ROI — so lifting quality on a high-volume publisher and scaling a high-quality one are both real ways to grow the program. Your engagement read nudges the score ±7%; the levers on each row follow the diagnosis.
